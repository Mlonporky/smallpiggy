Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: production 2D character cutout parts, transparent PNG.
Reference image: the attached approved pig design, specifically the middle three-quarter-right character. Keep exactly her short flush button nose, huge round cheeks, round pink belly, short limbs, dark red cape and pig face clasp, warm hand-painted watercolor style, eye design and proportions.
Create ONE transparent atlas with five separated items, NO text, NO backdrop, NO shadow, generous empty gaps.
LEFT HALF: a single connected CORE of the middle pig: head, neck, cape collar and pig clasp, full round torso, tail and cape all painted as ONE continuous intact unit in the standing three-quarter-right pose. Remove BOTH arms and BOTH legs cleanly; paint continuous pink torso underneath. NO floating head, NO shoulder holes. Bottom of torso is rounded and intact.
RIGHT HALF: four separated small limb cutouts, in a 2x2 arrangement. Upper row: near arm then far arm. Each arm is ONE short straight plump capsule, rounded pink shoulder cap at top and small brown cloven hoof at bottom, absolutely no bent elbow. These are rigid single-bone parts rotating from their top. Near arm belongs on viewer LEFT of core; far on viewer RIGHT. Identical skin, light and brushwork. Arm length about one third of torso height, thick cute proportions. Lower row: two short pink legs each including a small brown cloven foot, round hip cap at top, legs point straight down, flat sole at bottom. All parts at the same pixel scale as core, ready to assemble. No duplicate assembled character. No jewelry or acorn. Preserve real alpha transparency.
